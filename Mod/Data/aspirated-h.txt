# One entry per line, lowercase. Matching is by prefix, so "haut" also covers "haute",
# "hauteur" and "hautement". Prefer whole words: a short prefix over-matches, and "hero"
# would wrongly catch "heroine", which takes a mute h.
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# Do NOT list words with a mute h -- they elide normally and belong nowhere near this file:
# homme, heure, histoire, hopital, herbe, hiver, huile, honneur, heroine, hebergement.
#

# words introduced by a DLC or by another mod are covered before anyone reports them.
