# Grammatical gender of the species noun, independent of the pawn's sex.
#
# Format: <PawnKindDef defName>=f|m
# The defName is the game's own, in English, even where the label is translated.
#
# Without an entry here the game uses the pawn's sex as the noun's gender, which produces
# "le megaspider" for a male. With one, the generated article and possessive follow the noun.
#
# Every animal PawnKindDef shipped by RimWorld 1.6 and its expansions is listed: 115 entries,
# 25 feminine and 90 masculine. The gender is that of the French noun the official translation
# actually uses, read from Ludeon/RimWorld-fr rather than guessed from the English name - the
# choice of word decides the gender, and several are not the obvious cognate ("elk" is "wapiti",
# "megasloth" is "megatherium", "wolverine" is "glouton").
#
# Modded animals are not here and cannot be: add their defName to this file, no recompiling.
